A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Brice.

RVs have been part of my family’s life for generations. I’ve spent roughly 20 years around the RV industry, including manufacturing, sales, and service.

I started Michigan RV Repair to give West Michigan RV owners a straightforward, local option when something goes wrong. Whether your camper is at home, in storage, or at a campground, I believe in clear communication and taking pride in the work.

When you contact Michigan RV Repair, you’re reaching a local business that understands what your camper means to your family.
