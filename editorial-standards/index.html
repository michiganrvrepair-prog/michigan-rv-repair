MRR editorial standards

How Michigan RV Repair intends to keep owner education useful, transparent and accountable.

Who is behind MRR?

Michigan RV Repair is a local mobile RV service business. Its owner describes roughly 20 years of RV manufacturing, sales and service experience on the owner biography. This background informs the business's focus on practical repair and owner education. It does not establish manufacturer authorization, insurance licensing or independent product testing.

Content and technical review

MRR's guides are educational starting points. Before relying on a model-specific procedure or specification, consult the relevant manufacturer documentation and qualified personnel. Technical guides should identify the source of specific claims and distinguish general information from verified field experience. No individual review or certification is claimed unless expressly identified and documented.

Advertising and referral links

MRR may receive compensation or referral rewards from eligible links. Retailers and rental providers control their own inventory, prices and terms. Paid relationships should be disclosed near relevant links. MRR does not represent itself as the seller or rental operator when referring customers elsewhere. See advertising disclosures.

Insurance content

Insurance information is educational only and should direct readers to appropriately licensed professionals and authoritative regulatory sources. MRR does not quote, sell, bind or advise on insurance. Any referral compensation is subject to applicable law and program terms.

Photographs, corrections and updates

Illustrations are not proof of completed repairs. Actual customer work should be published only with appropriate authorization and identifying details removed. If you identify a factual error, outdated link or unsafe ambiguity, contact MRR with the page address and supporting documentation. MRR can review and correct the information.
