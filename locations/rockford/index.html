RV help for Rockford owners

For campers parked near home or at an accessible storage location, we help organize repairs without an unnecessary trip to a shop.

Every camper is different, so we start by learning what your RV is doing and where it is parked. When you request service, include your location, RV model, symptoms, and a few clear photos if possible.

Common services

Electrical & battery issues

12V problems, shore power and charging-system concerns.

Roof leaks & sealing

Inspecting roof seams, leak symptoms and weather-related damage.

Seasonal preparation

Winterization and spring preparation for Michigan weather.

What to expect

Tell us the RV location and the symptoms. We will discuss whether an on-site visit is suitable and confirm the details before the appointment. Travel availability and fees, if applicable, are clarified during scheduling.
