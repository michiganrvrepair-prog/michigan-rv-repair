RV insurance resources for Michigan owners

Prepare questions for a licensed insurance professional about your motorhome, fifth wheel or travel trailer. This is educational information, not an insurance quote or coverage recommendation.

Start with the RV and how it is used

Before speaking with a licensed provider, identify whether your RV is motorized or towable, its model year, make, model, ownership or financing status and typical use. Explain whether it is stored seasonally, used for occasional camping, occupied for extended periods or rented to others. These circumstances may affect what the provider needs to evaluate. Do not assume a tow vehicle's policy addresses every situation.

Questions to ask about a policy

  • What liability and physical damage coverage is included, and what is excluded?
  • How are deductibles, limits and the RV's value determined under the contract?
  • How does the policy address belongings, storage, roadside assistance or travel away from home?
  • Are there restrictions on full-time use, rental activity or modifications?
  • What documents and reporting steps are required if damage occurs?

Coverage names alone do not establish what is insured. Read the policy language and ask the licensed provider to explain exclusions and conditions. MRR does not interpret policies, advise on limits or determine coverage.

Prepare documents privately

Have vehicle identification, registration, purchase or financing information and photographs ready if the provider requests them. Use the provider's secure process; do not send driver's-license details, policy numbers or underwriting information to MRR. For Michigan licensing or regulatory questions, consult the Michigan Department of Insurance and Financial Services or an appropriately licensed professional.
