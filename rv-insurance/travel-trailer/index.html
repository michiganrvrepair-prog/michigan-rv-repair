Travel trailer and camper insurance questions

A towable RV presents different questions from a motorhome. Use this checklist to prepare for a licensed insurance discussion, not to select coverage on your own.

Ask about the trailer separately from the tow vehicle

Provide the trailer's make, model, year, use and ownership status to the licensed provider. Ask how relevant contracts address the trailer while attached to the tow vehicle, parked at a campsite and stored. Do not infer coverage from the tow vehicle's insurance card or a friend's policy. Ask for written clarification of what applies to your circumstances.

Seasonal storage and damage

Explain where the trailer is stored, whether it is used during winter, and whether you have installed accessories or made substantial modifications. Ask how the policy treats theft, weather damage, water intrusion, maintenance-related damage and personal belongings. Insurance and warranties are different contracts; neither should be assumed to cover a particular repair without reviewing its terms.

Financing, guests and rental use

If the trailer is financed, ask the lender and licensed provider what documentation and contractual requirements apply. If other people use the trailer or you intend to rent it out, disclose that use and ask how it affects the contracts. For a rented trailer, review the rental agreement and ask the rental provider about its protection terms and reporting procedures.

If you discover damage

  • Prioritize safety and stop using unsafe equipment.
  • Document visible damage if safe to do so.
  • Contact the appropriate insurer, owner or rental provider for instructions.
  • Keep estimates and records; do not assume a repair is covered or authorized.
