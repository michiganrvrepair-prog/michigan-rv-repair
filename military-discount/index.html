Thank you for your service.

We appreciate the men and women who have served our country. Our year-round military appreciation program is one way we can give back to those who serve and have served.

10% off eligible RV repair labor

Save up to $100 per service visit in Grand Rapids and throughout our West Michigan mobile service area. Terms apply.

REQUEST MOBILE RV SERVICE ↗CALL 616-290-3592

Who qualifies?

  • Active-duty U.S. military members
  • Veterans
  • National Guard members
  • Reserve members
  • Military retirees

There is no membership fee or separate enrollment requirement.

How to claim your discount

  1. Request service and select the optional military discount field, or mention the program when calling.
  2. Provide valid proof of eligibility for visual verification before approving your estimate. Please do not email or upload military IDs or service documents.
  3. MRR applies the eligible discount to your invoice after verification.

What is included?

Eligible MRR RV repair labor, including diagnostic labor. The discount is calculated on eligible labor charges before taxes and is limited to $100 per service visit.

For example, $400 in eligible labor qualifies for $40 off; $1,200 in eligible labor qualifies for the maximum $100 off.

What is excluded?

Parts, materials, supplies, travel, mileage, dispatch fees, taxes, third-party services and other non-labor charges are not eligible. The discount cannot be applied to previously issued invoices.

Program terms

Available year-round for eligible customers receiving MRR mobile RV service in West Michigan. One military discount per service visit; maximum $100 off eligible labor. Not transferable or redeemable for cash. Eligibility must be verified. Appointments and service scope are subject to availability and an approved estimate.

Other offers: The military discount cannot be combined with another discount. If you qualify for multiple MRR offers, we will apply the eligible offer that provides the greater savings. The separate RoadLife Savings Passport has its own terms and one-time voucher limit.

Reliable RV Repair, wherever your RV is parked.

We come to your driveway, campground or storage location. Request service or contact us with questions about eligibility.
