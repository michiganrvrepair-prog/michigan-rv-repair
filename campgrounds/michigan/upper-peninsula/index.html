Upper Peninsula RV camping guide

Build a route through the Upper Peninsula around confirmed campsite access and realistic travel distances.

Choose the style of camping

Decide whether you need a developed site with hookups or can manage a rustic location. The Upper Peninsula includes urban-adjacent sites, waterfront parks and remote forest areas. Check each operator's suitability for the length and height of your specific RV.

Reserve through official sources

Check Michigan DNR ↗ for state-managed locations, Recreation.gov ↗ for federal reservation listings and individual campground operators for private facilities. The Michigan atlas can help with discovery; verify all records independently.

Plan for seasonal limitations

Operating dates, roads, water availability and weather can differ across the peninsula. Check local conditions before travel; plan fuel stops and waste disposal without assuming every campground provides them. Keep an alternative campsite in mind.

Rental and repair preparedness

Confirm rental owner permission for your route and camping facilities. Carry emergency contact information and know the approved response for an equipment issue. MRR direct mobile coverage is based in West Michigan. See UP rental planning.
