West Michigan RV camping guide

Explore campground types and plan access, hookups and reservations for a West Michigan trip.

Choose an area

Consider lakeshore stops near Holland, Grand Haven and Muskegon or inland locations convenient to Grand Rapids. The type of site matters: park campsites, private RV parks and more rustic sites may have different utility and access arrangements.

Official reservation sources

Start with Michigan DNR ↗ for state-managed camping and Recreation.gov ↗ for applicable federal facilities. For county and private locations, confirm through the operator's own page. Use the MRR directory to discover potential stops.

Confirm campsite fit

Ask for maximum RV length, site dimensions, electric connection, water and dump facilities, quiet hours and any restrictions on delivery or extra vehicles. Verify your dates and actual rates; no live inventory or discount is promised here.

Nearby service planning

Keep campground contact details and your RV equipment labels at hand. For eligible locations, MRR offers mobile service by appointment. Rental customers should contact the owner first. Lake Michigan RV rentals.

Need mobile RV repair in West Michigan?

Send the RV year, make, model, its location and a photo of the affected component. MRR will discuss the scope, travel and diagnostic charges before scheduling; availability is not guaranteed.
