RV Refrigerator Not Cooling

RV refrigerators have different operating modes and cooling technologies. Power, ventilation, control faults, and operating conditions matter.

What might be happening?

RV refrigerators have different operating modes and cooling technologies. Power, ventilation, control faults, and operating conditions matter.

What to check or record before calling

Record the refrigerator make and model, selected operating mode, error codes, and how long it has been running.

Write down the RV year and model, the affected component's make and model, any error codes, and when the symptoms first began. Clear photos help when it is safe to take them.

Safety: Turn the unit off if you suspect leaking ammonia, smoke, or overheating. If a situation feels unsafe, stop using the affected equipment and seek qualified help.
