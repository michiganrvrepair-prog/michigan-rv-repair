RV Water Pump Runs but No Water

An empty tank, an open winterization valve, a blocked inlet, or air entering the suction side can interrupt flow.

What might be happening?

An empty tank, an open winterization valve, a blocked inlet, or air entering the suction side can interrupt flow.

What to check or record before calling

Confirm tank level and document which fixtures receive water.

Write down the RV year and model, the affected component's make and model, any error codes, and when the symptoms first began. Clear photos help when it is safe to take them.

Safety: Shut off the pump if it runs dry for an extended period. If a situation feels unsafe, stop using the affected equipment and seek qualified help.
