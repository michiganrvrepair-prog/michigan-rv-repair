How to Document an RV Roof Leak

Water can travel inside RV construction before it becomes visible. Record where moisture appears and when it occurs.

What might be happening?

Water can travel inside RV construction before it becomes visible. Record where moisture appears and when it occurs.

What to check or record before calling

Take safe photos of interior staining, exterior seams visible from the ground, and recent weather conditions.

Write down the RV year and model, the affected component's make and model, any error codes, and when the symptoms first began. Clear photos help when it is safe to take them.

Safety: Avoid climbing onto a wet or unstable roof. If a situation feels unsafe, stop using the affected equipment and seek qualified help.
