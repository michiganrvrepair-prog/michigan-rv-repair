Our approach

MRR aims to make website content usable for people with different abilities, devices and assistive technologies. Our ongoing work includes legible contrast, keyboard-reachable links, descriptive form labels, responsive layouts and alternative text for meaningful images.

This statement is a commitment to improve access, not a certification that every existing page or third-party platform meets a particular accessibility standard. Third-party systems, including payment providers, the Pillar customer portal and Etsy, maintain their own accessible experiences and support channels.

Request assistance

If a page, document, photograph or form prevents you from requesting service, please contact service@michigan-rv-repair.com or 616-290-3592. Describe the page or task and the accessible format or alternative method that would help. MRR will work to provide a reasonable alternative or correction.
