Product Promotions & Affiliate Disclosures

We want RV owners to know when information is educational, when a product is sold by someone else, and when MRR could be compensated for a referral.

Ask a product question Current purchase status

Updated November 25, 2026. The current MRR RV parts marketplace is a preview; it is not accepting online orders. Insurance quotes, paid university enrollment and campground membership packages are also not active. This policy describes our approach to product information and possible future partnerships.

1. Advertising and sponsorships

References to manufacturers, product categories, retailers, service providers or other organizations do not mean they sponsor, approve, certify or partner with Michigan RV Repair. Unless a particular page expressly identifies an approved relationship, no affiliation or endorsement is implied. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

2. Affiliate links and commissions

MRR may participate in affiliate or referral programs, including CJ Affiliate Deep Link Automation on the partner resources page. When a recommendation or shopping link could earn MRR compensation, we identify that potential material connection clearly and conspicuously near the applicable recommendation or link, not only in a footer or this policy page. A typical notice would read: “MRR may earn a commission if you purchase through this link.” Not every outside link is an affiliate link.

3. Products, suitability and comparisons

Illustrations, concept products, specifications, prices and descriptions on this website may be examples, not current offers. We do not claim to have personally tested a product unless we did. We do not publish unverified guarantees, unsupported superlatives, invented comparison results or claims of universal compatibility. Consult current manufacturer instructions, model numbers and independent qualified technicians before selecting or installing parts, particularly components affecting electricity, LP gas, brakes, structure and occupant safety.

4. Photos, branding and independent opinions

Commercial product photos, advertising creative, logos and vendor catalog content should appear only when owned by MRR, properly licensed, provided by an approved partner under its terms, or otherwise legally authorized. Editorial commentary is independent unless a sponsorship is specifically disclosed. If we receive samples, discounts or other benefits that could affect a recommendation, those relationships will be disclosed.

5. Reviews and ratings

MRR does not purchase or create fabricated consumer reviews. We will not present AI-generated or other fictitious personal experiences as real customer testimonials, and we will not offer incentives conditioned on a positive review. Campground listings are for discovery, not endorsements or a promise of booking availability or special rates.

6. Who handles orders, shipping and warranties?

For any active third-party link, the destination seller will display its own pricing, taxes, payment options, shipping, return and warranty terms. MRR does not control an independent merchant's checkout. If MRR itself sells a product or is the seller through an external storefront such as Etsy, MRR retains whatever seller responsibilities apply under law; these cannot be eliminated by a website disclaimer.

7. Insurance and regulated products

No MRR insurance application, quote or sale is currently active. Information regarding insurance companies or their programs is general and not a recommendation of a particular policy. MRR is not holding itself out as a licensed insurance producer. The external Truvo referral link is informational and does not activate MRR insurance quoting or sales. Any referral compensation is subject to applicable licensing and compensation rules and the provider's program terms. Eligibility and compensation have not been verified. Other insurance referral programs remain inactive.

8. Corrections, questions and consumer rights

See our current transaction status and privacy notice. To question a statement, request a correction, or ask about the source of a product recommendation, email service@michigan-rv-repair.com. Please do not send payment-card information, government IDs, or insurance application details by ordinary email. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Before MRR activates paid partnerships, direct product sales or regulated offerings, the applicable program agreement, product claims, merchant disclosures, taxes and licensing should be reviewed.
