Suburban RV furnace and water heater parts

An appliance with a heating or ignition fault needs diagnosis before replacement controls or gas components are ordered.

Find the appliance model

Read an accessible appliance rating plate and note its model and serial information. Furnace and water heater model families can have different controls, electrical ratings and vent arrangements. Check the applicable manual and current approved part numbers.

Furnace does not heat

Tell the technician whether the thermostat calls for heat, the blower starts, ignition occurs or the unit stops. Record any error indications and recent fuel or battery problems. Do not repeatedly attempt ignition if you smell propane; leave the area and seek appropriate emergency help.

Water heater problems

Identify whether the appliance supports gas, electric or multiple operating modes. Do not energize a dry tank or bypass a safety device. For a leak, distinguish the plumbing connection from the actual appliance before requesting parts. Water heater service.
