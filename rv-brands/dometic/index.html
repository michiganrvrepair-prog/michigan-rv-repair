Dometic RV appliance repair and parts guide

Collect the appliance's actual identification and operating symptoms before choosing a replacement part.

Identify the appliance and its energy source

Record the appliance model, product or serial number and any visible rating label. A refrigerator, air conditioner or water-system component may have multiple versions. Identify the power source or operating mode from its documentation. A similar-looking board or thermostat may not be compatible with the actual appliance.

Refrigerator symptoms

Note whether the unit fails to cool in every supported operating mode, displays an error, or has an intermittent problem. Record ambient conditions, recent changes and whether the RV has been stored. Do not disassemble gas or electrical systems or continue operating equipment with a suspected leak or overheating. See refrigerator troubleshooting.

Air conditioner symptoms

Describe whether the fan operates, whether the thermostat responds and whether the breaker trips. Airflow restrictions, power problems and controls can produce symptoms that resemble a failed cooling unit. Refrigerant and mains electrical work require appropriate qualifications. See RV air conditioner repair.

Before ordering a part

  • Use the complete appliance label, not just the RV year.
  • Check approved replacement part numbers and any supersession information.
  • Confirm electrical ratings, mounting and connector compatibility.
  • Review the retailer's return terms before opening or installing a component.
