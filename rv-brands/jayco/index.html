Jayco RV repair and replacement parts

Independent service guidance for Jayco camper and motorhome owners.

Start with the specific RV configuration

Record the model year, model designation and floor plan. Appliances and hardware can vary by configuration, so use the affected component's label and part number rather than assuming that every Jayco RV uses the same replacement. Keep any original manuals and service records available.

Water system and seasonal concerns

After storage, note whether a leak appears only when connected to city water, when the onboard pump runs, or when a fixture is used. Stop using the water system if an active leak is causing damage. Before ordering a fitting, identify the tubing and connection system. If the camper has been winterized, follow the manufacturer's recommissioning instructions. See our RV plumbing parts guide and spring preparation service.

Appliances and exterior equipment

For cooling, heating or awning problems, note the operating conditions, error indications and model labels. A thermostat issue, power-supply problem or control fault can resemble a failed appliance. Do not replace expensive components before diagnosis. For propane odor or an electrical burning smell, stop using the affected equipment and seek qualified help.

Before arranging repairs

  • Check warranty or service-contract authorization requirements.
  • Photograph the equipment label and visible symptoms.
  • Confirm the RV is accessible at its parking location.
  • Ask about diagnosis, travel and parts ordering before approving work.
