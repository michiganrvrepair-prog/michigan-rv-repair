Keystone camper repair and parts guide

Use the actual component model and RV build records to prepare for Keystone camper service.

Travel trailers and fifth wheels

Record the floor plan, model year and vehicle type. For a fifth-wheel, distinguish the living-area issue from hitch, frame or running-gear concerns. Do not tow if a structural or coupling issue is suspected; consult a qualified provider.

Roof, seals and water intrusion

A roof leak can travel through framing before showing indoors. Record the weather, affected area and previous sealant work. Identify the roof substrate through documentation rather than guessing from appearance. Roof sealant guide.

Leveling and slide-out equipment

Record accessible component labels and symptoms with the RV stationary. Leveling and room-extension faults can require different diagnostic steps; do not force movement, improvise supports or work underneath an unsupported RV.

Documentation and warranty
