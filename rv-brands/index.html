Why the component label matters

Two campers with the same floor plan may have different appliances, slide mechanisms or electrical equipment. Record the RV year, model and the affected component's manufacturer, model and serial number. The RV brand alone is rarely sufficient to identify a compatible replacement.

Independent mobile service

MRR is an independent mobile repair business, not an authorized dealer or warranty representative for these brands unless separately confirmed. For a warranty claim, ask the manufacturer or contract administrator which service providers and authorization steps are required before work begins. See our RV parts guide and repair services.
