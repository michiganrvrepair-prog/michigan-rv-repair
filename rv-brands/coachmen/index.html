Coachmen RV repair and parts identification

Coachmen RVs can share model names across configurations; identify the actual installed system before buying a part.

Start with the build information

Record model year, floor plan, VIN from your ownership documents and the component label. Keep the VIN private except when needed by the repair provider or manufacturer. A replacement listed for a similar Coachmen layout is not necessarily appropriate for your unit.

Slide rooms and power

A slide that works in only one direction may involve the controls, wiring, power supply or mechanism rather than the motor alone. Record the direction of failure and whether other 12-volt loads function. Stop if the room binds or shifts; do not bypass breakers or force the slide. Slide-out service.

Water and seasonal maintenance

For leaks, identify whether the supply is city water or the onboard pump. Document which fixture is in use and where the water appears. Check the RV's own winterization instructions before use after storage. Plumbing parts.
