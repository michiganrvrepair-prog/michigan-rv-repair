02 / IMMEDIATE SAFETY

If there's danger, don't wait for a technician.

Mobile RV repair is not a substitute for fire, medical, or utility emergency response.

Call 911 for an immediate emergency ↗
Propane smell or suspected leak

Leave the RV. Avoid flames, switches and anything that could create a spark. Call emergency services from a safe location.

Smoke, fire, arcing, or exposed live wiring

Keep clear of the hazard. Evacuate if needed and contact emergency responders.

Suspected carbon monoxide exposure

Get everyone outside into fresh air and call 911. Do not re-enter until professionals say it's safe.

RV unsafe to drive

Do not drive with an unsecured slide-out, awning, roof or other unsafe condition. Ask about recovery or towing options.
