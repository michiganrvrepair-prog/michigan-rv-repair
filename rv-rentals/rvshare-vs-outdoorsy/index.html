RVshare vs Outdoorsy: how to compare rentals

Compare the same trip on both independent rental marketplaces rather than choosing from a headline nightly rate.

Referral disclosure: MRR may receive a referral reward from eligible transactions. Rental inventory, bookings and terms are managed by the independent providers, not MRR. Advertising disclosures.
Browse RVshare ↗Browse Outdoorsy ↗

Compare like-for-like listings

Use the same dates, pickup radius, RV type, sleeping capacity and delivery requirements. A motorhome and a delivered trailer may solve different travel needs. Check the actual listing and written agreement; MRR has not independently tested or ranked either marketplace.

Calculate your full trip cost

Compare the final rental quote, not just the displayed nightly rate. Add campground fees, fuel, travel to pickup, mileage or generator charges, cleaning, delivery and other disclosed fees. Confirm deposits, cancellation terms and any return-condition charges. A refundable deposit affects cash needed even if it is not ultimately a trip expense.

Compare the owner and handoff

Ask about pickup times, vehicle condition, included equipment and a systems demonstration. For a delivered trailer, confirm campground approval and the owner's setup and retrieval responsibilities. For a motorhome, verify designated travel seating, driving requirements and route restrictions.

Check protection and support

Read each rental agreement's actual protection terms and exclusions. Ask who to contact for roadside problems, damage and equipment failure. Do not assume that your personal insurance covers a rental; ask an appropriately licensed provider if needed.

  • Save comparable written quotes.
  • Verify campground reservations separately.
  • Review cancellation and return requirements.
  • Confirm owner authorization before arranging any repair.
