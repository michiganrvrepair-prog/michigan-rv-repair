RVshare rental guide for Michigan

Use this independent guide from Michigan RV Repair to prepare for a motorhome or travel trailer rental through RVshare.

How to narrow your RVshare search

Start with your preferred pickup area, travel dates, number of travelers and whether you want to drive, tow or request delivery. If your trip begins near Grand Rapids, compare pickup locations around the city and along your planned route. Availability varies by listing and date; MRR does not maintain live rental inventory.

Read the listing like an RV owner

Sleeping capacity is not the same as the number of designated travel seats. Check seat belts, bed dimensions, storage and accessibility. Ask the owner whether the water heater, refrigerator, air conditioner and furnace are operational and which systems require shore power, propane or generator use. If you plan to camp without hookups, ask about battery capacity and charging arrangements rather than assuming every appliance will work off-grid.

Pickup versus campground delivery

Delivery can simplify a first trip with a travel trailer if the owner offers it. Confirm the delivery radius, setup and retrieval fees, campsite access and whether the campground allows deliveries. If driving a motorhome, review height and length restrictions along the route. For towing, verify vehicle and hitch ratings and ask about brake controller and electrical connector requirements.

What to confirm before booking

  • Final price, deposit, included mileage and generator allowances.
  • Cancellation, protection and roadside assistance terms.
  • Owner contact, pickup time and return instructions.
  • Campground reservations, hookups and RV length limits.

Take time-stamped photos at pickup and return, and follow the rental provider's procedures if a problem occurs. Do not modify or repair a rental without the required authorization.

Need help with your own RV in West Michigan?
