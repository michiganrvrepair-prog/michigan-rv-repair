Camper rentals with campground delivery

A delivered travel trailer can simplify a stationary camping trip when an owner offers delivery and the campground permits it.

What delivery includes

Delivery arrangements vary by listing. Ask whether the owner transports the trailer, levels it, connects permitted utilities, demonstrates equipment and returns to retrieve it. Do not assume that every listing includes setup, tank emptying or linens. Obtain a written total including delivery distance, setup, pickup and any extra charges.

Confirm campground approval

Contact the park before booking. Some sites have narrow approaches, short pads or limited hours. Ask whether a third-party rental owner can enter, whether you must be present, and whether the park requires a vehicle pass or advance registration. Provide the owner with the site number, arrival window, road restrictions and confirmed hookups. A campground reservation is separate from the rental agreement.

Coordinate utilities and daily use

Ask what electrical connection the trailer requires, whether water is available at the site, and what the owner expects you to do with wastewater. If there is no sewer connection, agree on tank-use and emptying procedures before arrival. Follow the owner's operating instructions; never improvise electrical connections or propane equipment.

Delivery and pickup checklist

  • Confirm arrival and departure times with the owner and campground.
  • Verify full trailer length, slide clearance and access route.
  • Document condition at handoff and ask who to call for equipment issues.
  • Confirm cleaning, trash removal and tank procedures before retrieval.

If equipment fails, contact the rental provider or owner rather than authorizing repairs yourself. Explore RV trailer types and ownership questions or return to the Michigan rental guide to compare self-pickup options.
