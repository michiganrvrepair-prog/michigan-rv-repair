RV rentals in Michigan

Plan a weekend at a West Michigan campground or a longer trip through the Upper Peninsula. Compare independent RV rental marketplaces and use MRR’s practical planning guide before booking.

Find a motorhome or camper rental

Start with the type of trip you want, not just the nightly price. A motorhome combines driving and living space, while a towable camper requires a compatible tow vehicle or delivery to the campsite. RVshare and Outdoorsy are independent rental marketplaces; check each listing for pickup location, dates, equipment and restrictions.

Compare the full cost of an RV rental

Enter your actual travel dates and pickup area on each marketplace. Compare the complete quote, including taxes, platform fees, mileage, generator usage, cleaning, delivery, protection options and any deposit. A lower nightly rate may not mean a lower total. Ask whether bedding, cookware, hoses, leveling equipment and electrical adapters are included.

Choose an RV that fits your Michigan campsite

Confirm the campground's maximum RV length, electrical service, water and sewer access, arrival time and seasonal operating dates. For a delivered trailer, confirm the campground permits third-party delivery and that the owner can set up and retrieve the unit. For a towable rental, verify the loaded trailer weight, tongue weight, vehicle payload and all applicable towing ratings before committing.

Before leaving the pickup location

  • Walk through the RV with the owner and document existing damage.
  • Learn how to operate the water, electrical and waste systems using the owner's instructions.
  • Confirm roadside assistance contacts and what to do if an appliance fails.
  • Review return requirements, fuel, tank emptying and cancellation terms.

Does MRR rent RVs directly?

No. MRR provides local mobile repair and educational resources. Rental agreements, availability, payments, deposits and claims are handled by the rental provider and owner. No rental inventory or prices are offered by MRR.

Need help with your own RV in West Michigan?

Michigan RV Repair provides mobile RV repair in Grand Rapids and surrounding West Michigan communities. For a rental, contact the owner or rental provider for authorization before arranging repairs. For your own RV, request a diagnosis or installation estimate.
