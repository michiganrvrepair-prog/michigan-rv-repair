Match your rental to the trip

For a family staying at one campground, a delivered trailer may be convenient if offered by the owner. For a multi-stop road trip, a motorhome or campervan may suit a different travel style. Compare listings for your actual travel dates and departure area. Check floor plans, bed dimensions, travel seating, pet rules and accessibility rather than relying on the advertised sleeping count alone.

Questions for the Outdoorsy owner

Ask what is included in the rental and what you must bring. Confirm how the refrigerator, water heater, furnace and air conditioner operate, and whether the owner provides a sewer hose, potable-water hose, electrical cord and leveling equipment. Ask which hookups are needed for the equipment you plan to use. For a winter or shoulder-season Michigan trip, ask whether the RV is winterized and what water-system restrictions apply.

Plan the campsite before committing

Reserve a site that can accommodate the RV's full length and access requirements. Confirm the campground's electrical service, water availability, check-in rules and any third-party delivery policy. When driving, account for the RV's actual height and width. When towing, verify loaded weights, payload, hitch compatibility and braking requirements.

Compare costs and return requirements

Check the complete quote, including mileage, delivery, cleaning, protection, taxes, platform fees and deposits. Read the cancellation and damage-reporting procedures. At pickup, have the owner demonstrate the equipment, document its condition and provide instructions for emergencies. Follow the rental agreement and get authorization before arranging any repair.

Michigan RV Repair provides mobile RV repair in Grand Rapids and surrounding West Michigan communities. For a rental, contact the owner or rental provider for authorization before arranging repairs. For your own RV, request a diagnosis or installation estimate.
