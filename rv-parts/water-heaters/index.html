RV water heater parts and replacement guide

A replacement water heater must match the RV installation and approved utility connections.

Identify the heater

Record manufacturer and model, energy modes, installation opening and accessible rating details. Tank and tankless designs differ in operating and installation requirements. Do not assume matching exterior doors mean matching assemblies.

Leak or no hot water?

Note whether leaks appear under city-water pressure, when the pump operates or when heating. A failed heating cycle does not identify its cause. Do not operate a leaking or dry heater, bypass temperature controls or attempt manual ignition.

Parts versus complete replacement

Identify approved anode, drain, control, heating or other service parts only for the exact model. Some units use different tank materials and manufacturer maintenance requirements. Cross-model substitution can be unsafe.

Installation and return questions

Confirm clearances, venting, mounting, plumbing and electrical or fuel requirements with the installer. Check retailer returns before opening parts. Use water heater service for diagnosis and Suburban guidance where applicable.
