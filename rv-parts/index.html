How to identify the correct RV part

Photograph the component label, existing connections and mounting location without disassembling energized or hazardous equipment. Record part number, manufacturer, model, serial number, dimensions and ratings. Use manufacturer compatibility information or ask the retailer to verify a replacement. A visually similar part can have different electrical, mechanical or material specifications.

When to diagnose before buying

A water pump that runs without delivering water may have an air leak, supply problem or obstruction. A battery that does not charge may have a wiring, fuse, battery or charging-system issue. Replacing the first component that seems related can waste money. Use our RV problem guides or request an inspection.
