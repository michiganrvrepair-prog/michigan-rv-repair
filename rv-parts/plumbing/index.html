RV plumbing parts and water-system components

Identify the actual leak or water-system fault before purchasing a replacement. RV plumbing components vary by manufacturer, model and installation.

Water pumps and fresh-water components

Record the pump model, voltage, manufacturer specifications, connection type and mounting arrangement. Check whether the issue is a supply restriction, leak, power problem or pump failure before ordering a new pump. A pump that cycles frequently may indicate a leak or pressure loss elsewhere. Use parts rated for the intended potable-water application where required.

Fittings, valves and hoses

Match original tubing size, fitting system, material and pressure and temperature ratings. Similar-looking fittings may not be interchangeable. Identify whether the component serves fresh water, wastewater or another system; do not substitute a generic fitting without confirming suitability. For water heater connections, follow appliance manufacturer specifications and avoid modifying gas or electrical components.

RV toilet and waste-system parts

Find the toilet manufacturer, model and original part number before buying seals, valves or hardware. A bowl that does not hold water may require diagnosis of the seal or another component rather than replacement of the whole toilet. Check retailer compatibility information and return policy. Waste-system repairs require appropriate sanitation precautions.

Before contacting a parts supplier

  • Photograph the component label and accessible connections.
  • Record fitting type, measurements, material and original part number.
  • Describe whether the problem occurs on city water, onboard pump or both.
  • Check manufacturer-approved replacement information and retailer return conditions.
