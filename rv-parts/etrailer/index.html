etrailer RV and towing equipment guide

Research towing and trailer equipment before buying. This independent guide explains the measurements and ratings to verify when comparing products from retailers such as etrailer.

Program status: MRR has not activated an etrailer referral link. This is an independent educational guide, not an etrailer storefront, endorsement, discount offer or authorized dealer claim.

Hitch and tow vehicle compatibility

Begin with the exact tow vehicle year, make, model, trim and configuration. Check the owner's manual and vehicle labels for applicable towing and payload limits. Compare the loaded trailer weight and tongue weight with the vehicle, hitch, receiver and related equipment ratings; the lowest applicable limit governs. Passenger weight, cargo and hitch hardware consume available payload. A higher-rated hitch does not increase the tow vehicle's rated capacity.

Trailer wiring and brake controllers

Identify the existing connector type and vehicle wiring configuration. Determine whether the trailer uses electric brakes and whether the tow vehicle has an appropriate factory or aftermarket brake controller. Check manufacturer instructions for the specific vehicle and trailer. Do not assume a connector adapter adds missing brake-control capability. Have damaged wiring, braking equipment and safety-critical installations inspected by a qualified technician.

Trailer accessories and cargo equipment

For racks, steps, jacks and stabilizers, measure the mounting space and check the manufacturer's approved application, load limits and clearance requirements. A stabilizer is not necessarily a lifting jack. Cargo carried behind an RV can change weight distribution, so verify the RV manufacturer's limits before installing a rack or carrier.

What to collect before comparing etrailer listings

  • Vehicle and trailer identification, equipment model and existing part numbers.
  • Actual loaded weights, tongue weight and all relevant ratings.
  • Receiver size, mounting dimensions and electrical connector type.
  • Manufacturer fitment instructions, return policy and installation requirements.
