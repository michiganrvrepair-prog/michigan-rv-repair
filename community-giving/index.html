ROOTED IN WEST MICHIGAN · COMMUNITY GIVING

More trees. Greener neighborhoods. A happier West Michigan.

At Michigan RV Repair, we love the outdoors—and we want the places our families camp, explore, and call home to thrive. Help us encourage tree planting and care in Wyoming, Michigan, starting with the important work of the City of Wyoming Tree Commission.

See how to help plant trees →Meet the Tree Commission ↗
COMMUNITY FIRST

Help Wyoming grow its urban tree canopy.

The City of Wyoming Tree Commission promotes programs that maintain and improve trees on public and private properties, encourage species diversity, and support a healthier, more beautiful city. Michigan RV Repair is helping spread the word about local tree care and ways neighbors can participate.

Contact the Tree Commission

Ask which current planting projects need volunteers or financial support and how contributions should be directed.

City Tree Commission ↗

Explore Wyoming's charitable giving outlet

The Greater Wyoming Community Resource Alliance supports community projects. Ask whether it can accept a designated contribution for a Tree Commission project before paying.

About the Alliance ↗

Volunteer and spread the word

Tree-planting days, watering, mulch, and community outreach make a real difference. Contact a local organizer to learn where help is needed.

Ask Tree Amigos about opportunities ↗
Donations go to the organization you choose—not to MRR. This page does not collect charitable funds. A Tree Commission-specific donation channel has not been independently confirmed. Please obtain confirmation from the City or the receiving organization before giving and ask about any project designation or tax receipt.
WHY WE CARE

From our RV family to our community.

Trees offer shade, support wildlife, beautify streets and parks, and help make outdoor spaces more inviting. Caring for Michigan's landscapes is a natural extension of what we love about RV life. Our goal is to promote practical local involvement—not to imply a formal city partnership or a donation match that hasn't been established.

Want to organize a business volunteer day or tree planting? Contact the local organization directly, then share the details with Michigan RV Repair at service@michigan-rv-repair.com.
