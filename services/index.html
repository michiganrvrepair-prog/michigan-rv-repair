SERVICE CALL POLICIES

STANDARD LABOR, TRAVEL & EMERGENCY RATES

01

Standard labor

$150 / hour

Weekday diagnostics, troubleshooting, repairs, and installations. One-hour minimum, then billed according to actual approved work.

02

Travel

$95 / trip

Flat travel charge when the service location is more than 15 miles from MRR's agreed dispatch point. No additional travel charge within 15 miles. Confirmed before booking.

03

Weekend emergency

$300 / hour

Double the standard hourly labor rate for approved emergency visits on Saturdays and Sundays. Availability must be confirmed by MRR.

04

Holiday emergency

$450 / hour

Triple the standard hourly labor rate for approved emergency visits on recognized holidays. Applies instead of—not in addition to—the weekend emergency multiplier.

Parts, materials, applicable taxes, and any agreed travel fee are additional unless expressly included in a written package quote. Diagnostic time is billed at the applicable labor rate. Weekend and holiday premiums apply to labor only; published packages require confirmation for emergency scheduling.

How these prices work

Published MRR rates: Standard labor $150/hour; travel over 15 miles $95; weekend emergency labor $300/hour; holiday emergency labor $450/hour. These do not stack. Starting prices above are proposed minimum labor/package allowances, not fixed all-in quotes.
