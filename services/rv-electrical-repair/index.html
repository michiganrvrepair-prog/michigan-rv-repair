RV Electrical Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

Lights going dim, outlets not working, or batteries that won’t charge? RV electrical issues can interrupt a trip in a hurry. We help West Michigan RV owners narrow down problems with shore power, converters, batteries, 12-volt circuits, and related components.

Request RV Electrical RepairCall 616-290-3592

Signs it's time to ask for help

Every RV has its quirks. These are some of the symptoms worth telling us about:

  • shore power is connected but 12V lights are dead
  • batteries are not charging
  • a breaker trips repeatedly
  • the converter hums but the battery stays low

How we can help

  • Shore-power and 12-volt system troubleshooting
  • Battery, converter and inverter diagnostics
  • Circuit, outlet, fuse and breaker troubleshooting

We explain what we find, discuss next steps, and help you make an informed decision. Repairs and parts depend on the RV and the issue.

From our RV family to yours

Michigan RV Repair is a locally operated mobile service rooted in years of hands-on RV manufacturing, sales, and service experience. We know how much a working camper matters to family plans, and we value clear communication and careful work.

Service is available by appointment in Grand Rapids and nearby West Michigan communities; contact us to confirm coverage for your location.

What to send us

Your RV year, make and model, parking location, a description of the problem, and photos of any relevant labels or visible damage.
