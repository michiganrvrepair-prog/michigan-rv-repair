Seasonal RV maintenance checklist

Use this noninteractive checklist to prepare for a technician and track the tasks appropriate to your RV.

Spring return to service

Review the winterization record, inspect visible damage and follow the RV manufacturer's instructions for recommissioning the water system. Ask qualified personnel to assess fuel, electrical, tires, brakes and other safety-critical systems before travel. De-winterization service.

During camping season

Record new water stains, unusual odors, power faults and changes in equipment operation. Keep roof and exterior seal inspection intervals consistent with manufacturer guidance; avoid walking on an unapproved roof. Do not ignore repeated breaker trips or abnormal slide movement.

Fall and storage

Arrange a winterization plan suited to the installed water system, storage location and climate. Confirm what work is included, keep documentation of services and check storage-provider requirements. Winterization service.

Keep your records

Maintain a list of component models, service dates and outstanding defects. Use free RoadLife membership if you want to organize records and opt into available benefits, or keep your own paper log. No automatic marketing enrollment is created by reading this checklist.

Need mobile RV repair in West Michigan?

Send the RV year, make, model, its location and a photo of the affected component. MRR will discuss the scope, travel and diagnostic charges before scheduling; availability is not guaranteed.
