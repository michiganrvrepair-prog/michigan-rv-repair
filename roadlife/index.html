Helpful starting points for routine planning and safety—not instructions for hazardous repairs. Consult manufacturers and qualified technicians as needed.

Four-season RV maintenance checklist
  • Spring: inspect seals, tires, batteries, brakes, and water systems according to the manufacturer.
  • Summer: check tire pressure cold, monitor roof and window seals, test detectors and inspect hookups.
  • Fall: prepare fresh-water, plumbing and heating systems for freezing conditions; follow model-specific winterization guidance.
  • Winter: review stored-RV ventilation, battery care, pest prevention and safe load conditions.
Buying a used RV? Start here
  • Confirm ownership and title details independently.
  • Request maintenance records and a pre-purchase inspection.
  • Look for moisture intrusion, tire age and roof concerns.
  • Check the vehicle and equipment for recall information.
